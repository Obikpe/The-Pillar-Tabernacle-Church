# Open Decisions and Information Required

This document prevents assumptions from becoming accidental requirements.

## 1. Church identity and content

- Official logo files and brand guidelines.
- Exact brand colours and typography, if defined.
- Official church address and map location.
- Service days and times.
- Official phone, email and social links.
- Approved history, mission, vision and statement of beliefs.
- Leadership names, titles, biographies and image permissions.
- Official livestream and sermon channels.
- Approved website domain and admin subdomain.

## 2. Membership policy

Agreed:
- Pastor and designated administrators can approve membership.
- Authorised approvers can approve directly.
- Department heads do not automatically receive approval authority.

Still to confirm:
- Exact meaning/criteria of formal membership.
- Whether rejection is a valid recorded outcome and who may reject.
- Whether an appeal or reconsideration process exists.
- Whether membership can be suspended, reinstated or transferred and who authorises each action.
- Who may designate/revoke membership approvers and whether the pastor alone controls this.
- Whether an approver may approve their own record (recommended: prohibit).
- What notification, if any, follows a decision.

## 3. Roles and administration

- Is there a church secretary role, and what may it do?
- Which department heads will have portal accounts?
- Which departments exist and who leads them?
- Can content editors be separate from department heads?
- Who is the initial system owner and account recovery contact?
- Who can create, disable and reset administrator accounts?
- Which roles may export member records?

## 4. Registration and visitor care

- Are email and birthday optional? (Current proposal: yes.)
- Is phone number mandatory?
- What exact visitor categories are needed?
- How is in-person attendance/visit verified, if at all?
- Who receives new-registration notifications?
- How many follow-up attempts are expected and over what period?
- Which staff may view follow-up notes?
- What is the church's WhatsApp group eligibility policy?

## 5. Prayer requests

- Who may review private requests?
- May requests be assigned to department prayer coordinators?
- What retention period applies?
- What wording should explain confidentiality and its limits?
- Will public prayer requests be displayed at all, or should the form remain private-only?
- Who may approve public publication?

## 6. Website content

- Who may publish announcements and events?
- Is a publishing approval step required?
- Which event fields are needed?
- Should events support registration in the first release?
- Which sermon metadata should be searchable?
- Who owns the church media accounts?

## 7. Privacy and retention

- Church-approved privacy notice and consent wording.
- Retention period for visitor submissions and inactive member records.
- Procedure for access/correction/deletion requests.
- Data controller contact and responsible privacy contact.
- Whether any information about minors will be collected. If yes, additional safeguards and a specific process are required.
- Vendor/data-hosting review and cross-border transfer considerations.

## 8. Infrastructure and budget

- Does the church already own a domain?
- Preferred hosting account owner.
- Supabase project/account owner.
- Acceptable recurring budget if free-tier limits are exceeded.
- Backup expectations.
- Preferred email provider, if/when email is introduced.

## 9. Scope boundary reminders

- Attendance remains manual-only and outside the initial release.
- Finance remains a future module.
- Automated email and WhatsApp are not initial-release features.
- No public members-only WhatsApp invitation.
- No deployment without explicit approval.

## 10. Decision recording

When a question is resolved:
1. Record the agreed answer here.
2. Update the relevant requirements/design document.
3. Add an Architecture Decision Record if it changes technology, system boundaries or a significant technical approach.
